# Mobile release notes — v2.96.4 (since v2.81.0)

These notes are written to match the tone of [`app.leather.io/changelog`](https://app.leather.io/changelog).

Swaps are currently feature-flagged, so swap-related changes are intentionally excluded.

## App Store / Play Store “What’s New”

- **USDCx now supported by default**: USDCx balances appear automatically in your wallet with accurate fiat pricing.
- **Token details are now available**: Explore richer token info across supported standards (including SIP-009).
- **Collectibles are now easier to explore**: Improved collectible details and previews for inscriptions and stamps.
- **Improved STX and BTC onramps**: Updated buy experience via Onramper with more providers and payment options.
- **App icon switcher**: Choose a new app icon.
- **Fixes & reliability**: General bug fixes and stability improvements.

## `app.leather.io/changelog` entry (richer)

### Leather now supports USDCx by default — plus improved token details and collectibles on mobile

USDCx now shows up automatically in your wallet with accurate fiat pricing, and we’ve expanded token details and collectibles on mobile so you can explore more of what you own with less setup.

#### USDCx now supported as a default asset

USDCx balances now appear automatically alongside your other assets, complete with fiat pricing—so there’s one less token you need to manually add.

#### Token details are now available

Token details are now ungated on mobile, with richer information across supported standards (including SIP-009).

#### Collectibles are now easier to explore

We’ve improved collectible details and previews (including inscriptions and stamps), making it easier to view and understand what you’re holding.

#### Improved STX and BTC onramps

Buying STX and BTC has been refreshed with Onramper, giving you access to more providers and payment methods depending on your region.

#### Fixes & reliability

A set of stability improvements and bug fixes to keep things running smoothly.
